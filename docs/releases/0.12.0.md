Mute and solo voices, seeking, and a C64 ROM setting.

## Downloads

- **ChipDeck-0.12.0-windows-x64.zip**: Windows 10/11 (64-bit). Unzip anywhere and run `chipdeck.exe` from the folder.
- **ChipDeck-0.12.0-android.apk**: Android. Installs over earlier versions as an update.
- **ChipDeck-0.12.0-SHA256SUMS.txt**: checksums of the two files above.

## What's new

- **Mute and solo voices**: click (tap) a channel's scope to mute it, right-click (long press) to hear only that channel, and again to bring the others back. Works for SID voices and digis, Atari YM channels and the STE DAC, every tracker channel of MOD/XM and TFMX, and TFMX-ST. Muted scopes are dimmed but keep moving, so you can still see what they play. "Unmute all" appears while anything is muted. Keys: 1-9 toggle channels 1-9, 0 unmutes all. Mutes stay when you change subtune and are cleared for the next tune.
- **Seeking**: drag or click the position bar to jump anywhere in a tune, in every format. Shift+Left/Right jump 10 seconds. On Android the notification and lock screen get a seek bar too, and so do the second-screen controls. SID tunes are fast-forwarded without sound, so a long jump can take a second or two.
- **C64 ROMs**: Settings has a new "C64 ROMs" section for the BASIC, KERNAL and character ROMs (pick each file, or "Find in folder…" to pick up the files from an emulator such as VICE). C64 BASIC tunes need the BASIC ROM; Now playing says so when it is missing. ChipDeck does not include or download ROMs.

## Known limitations

- Unmuting the SID "Digi" channel takes effect at the tune's next volume change (digi tunes change it all the time).
- TFMX-ST digital drums are a best guess.
