The first public release of ChipDeck, a retro chiptune player for Windows and Android.

## Downloads

- **ChipDeck-0.11.0-windows-x64.zip**: Windows 10/11 (64-bit). Unzip anywhere and run `chipdeck.exe` from the folder.
- **ChipDeck-0.11.0-android.apk**: Android. Copy it to the device and open it (allow "install unknown apps" for your file manager or browser). Signed with the same key as earlier test builds, so it installs over them as an update.
- **ChipDeck-0.11.0-SHA256SUMS.txt**: checksums of the two files above.

Tested on Windows and on an Ayn Thor (Android, landscape and dual screen). See the README for which other platforms build and which are untested.

## Formats

- **Commodore 64** `.sid` (PSID/RSID, 2SID/3SID) with reSIDfp emulation, 6581/8580 choice, HVSC Songlengths, and STIL/BUGlist tune info
- **Atari ST** `.sndh` and `.ym`, including STE DAC sound
- **Amiga** Protracker `.mod`, Chris Huelsbeck's **TFMX** (1.5, Pro, 7V), Jochen Hippel TFMX and Future Composer
- **Atari ST TFMX-ST** (ChipDeck's own port of the ST replay routine)
- **PC** Fasttracker 2 `.xm`

## Features so far

- **Music library**: add your music folders (HVSC, the SNDH archive, Modland and so on), browse by folder with tune counts, instant search by title, composer, year or folder, and a format filter grouped by system. Refreshes are incremental and only when you ask; unreadable or broken files are skipped and listed in a scan report.
- **Play straight from the library** without changing your playlist; next, previous and auto-advance follow the folder or search results you played from.
- **Playing Now, Favourites and your own playlists (new in 0.11.0)**: star tunes (F key), make named playlists, organise them in folders, reorder, rename, and add a tune, a folder or search results to any list.
- **Random order (new in 0.11.0)**: plays every tune of the list once in a random order (S key); previous steps back through what was already played.
- **Now playing**: title, composer, year, chip and format details, subtune picker, progress bar, volume, and "when a song ends" modes (all subtunes then next, next tune, repeat).
- **Visualiser** with one oscilloscope per channel: SID voices plus digi, Atari YM channels plus the STE DAC, every tracker channel of MOD/XM/TFMX.
- **Layouts** for wide desktop windows (Library, Now playing and Playlists side by side), phones, and short landscape screens such as the Ayn Thor.
- **Windows**: keyboard shortcuts (Space play/pause, Left/Right subtune, N/P tune, +/- volume, F favourite, S random order).
- **Android**: background playback with a notification, lock screen and headset controls, pause on headphone unplug, and player controls on a second screen on dual-screen devices.

## Known limitations

- Some damaged `.mod`/`.xm` files found in large archives do not load; they are listed in the scan report.
- TFMX-ST digital drums are a best guess.
- The Android second-screen controls have no favourite star yet; star the tune on the main screen.
